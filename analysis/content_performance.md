# Social Media Content Performance Analysis

## Objective
Demonstrate a repeatable way to compare content performance using reach, impressions, engagement rate and link clicks.

## Dataset
- 60 illustrative Instagram posts
- 6 brand labels: Mangiee and five anonymized competitors
- 10 posts per brand
- Content formats: Reels, carousels, static posts and stories
- All observations are synthetic sample data, not scraped or verified brand analytics.

## Method
1. Calculate engagements as likes + comments + shares + saves.
2. Calculate engagement rate as engagements divided by reach, multiplied by 100.
3. Compare average reach, impressions, engagement rate and link clicks by brand.
4. Segment results by content format to identify formats worth testing.
5. Avoid relying on a single metric: reach indicates distribution, while engagement rate indicates interaction relative to reach.

## Interpretation framework
- High reach + high engagement rate: strong candidate for repeatable content formats.
- High reach + low engagement rate: distribution is strong, but creative relevance or call-to-action may need review.
- Low reach + high engagement rate: content resonates with the reached audience; distribution may need improvement.
- Low reach + low engagement rate: review the hook, audience fit, creative quality and posting context.

## Limitations
The sample is intentionally illustrative. Real decisions require platform exports, consistent date windows, comparable account sizes, paid/organic labels, campaign context and a larger sample.

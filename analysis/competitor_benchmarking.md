# Competitor Benchmarking

## Scope
Five anonymized competitor labels were benchmarked alongside Mangiee using 10 illustrative posts per brand (60 posts total).

## Benchmark dimensions
- Average reach
- Average impressions
- Average engagement rate
- Average link clicks
- Most frequently observed content type in the sample

## How to use the comparison
1. Compare engagement rate alongside reach; do not rank brands on reach alone.
2. Review which formats appear frequently, then compare their average performance.
3. Identify under-served content themes and turn them into small experiments.
4. Validate each insight against actual platform analytics before making a campaign decision.

## Important data note
Competitor A–E are placeholders. The figures are synthetic and must not be represented as real competitor research. Replace the labels and data with verified public observations or authorized analytics before using this as a factual market benchmark.

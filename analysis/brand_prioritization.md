# Partnership Lead Prioritization

## Objective
Create a transparent scoring model to prioritize potential brand partnership outreach.

## Scoring model
Each factor is scored from 1 (low) to 5 (high).

| Factor | Weight | What to assess |
|---|---:|---|
| Branding needs | 35% | Clarity of positioning, visual consistency, content gaps and brand storytelling needs |
| Online presence | 30% | Website/social activity, content consistency and discoverability |
| Growth signals | 35% | New launches, hiring, expansion, campaigns or visible audience momentum |

**Weighted score (0–100)** = (Branding needs ÷ 5 × 35) + (Online presence ÷ 5 × 30) + (Growth signals ÷ 5 × 35)

## Suggested prioritization
- High: 75–100
- Medium: 60–74.9
- Low: below 60

## Outreach workflow
1. Build a prospect list from a defined category and geography.
2. Review public website and social profiles.
3. Record evidence and date checked for each score.
4. Prioritize high-scoring prospects for personalized outreach.
5. Track contacted, replied, meeting booked and outcome.

## Data note
Prospect names and scores in the sample CSV are fictional placeholders. Replace them with real prospects and evidence before outreach.
